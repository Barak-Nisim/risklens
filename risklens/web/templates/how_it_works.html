Methodology

How RiskLens works

Every step below is real: the same code that renders this page runs the scoring engine and, if you enable it, the AI layer. Nothing here is a marketing simplification of something different happening under the hood.

1

Answer, aligned to NIST CSF 2.0

The questionnaire is organized around the framework's six functions: Govern (risk strategy, vendor oversight, audit readiness), Identify (asset inventory, risk assessment), Protect (identity, MFA, access control, data security), Detect (monitoring), Respond (incident response), and Recover (business continuity). Each question is answered on the same 0–4 maturity scale, from "not started" to "optimized," so the whole assessment is directly comparable across every category.

2

Deterministic, weighted scoring

Every question, category, and function carries a weight reflecting how much it matters. Scores roll up as a plain weighted average at every level, question to category to function to overall. There is no hidden curve and no machine-learned model deciding the number. A weight-1.3 question on MFA enforcement pulls its category's score twice as hard as a weight-0.6 question, and you can recompute the whole result by hand from the numbers on the report.

3

Findings ranked by real business impact

Anything scoring below the maturity threshold becomes a finding. Findings are ranked by weight × (4 − score), so a high-weight, low-maturity gap, like unreassessed vendors, outranks a low-weight gap even if both are technically "below threshold." Priority order isn't a guess; it's the same formula every time.

4

AI narrates. It never re-scores.

When AI narration is enabled, Claude receives the already-computed scores and ranked findings as structured data and is explicitly instructed to narrate and prioritize them for a leadership audience, not to recompute or second-guess them. If you disagree with a finding's priority, the fix is in the scoring math above, not in the AI layer. This split is deliberate: the deterministic core works, and is fully tested, with zero dependency on any AI service.

5

Ships to where the work happens

The prioritized findings export as a CSV with the columns Jira's own importer expects, so the backlog goes straight into your team's actual board instead of sitting in a report nobody revisits.
