What it does

Deterministic scoring

A weighted scoring engine, not a black box. Every score is explainable and can be recomputed by hand from the numbers on the page.

AI-narrated exec summary

Claude turns the scored findings into a leadership-facing summary and remediation plan. It narrates the findings. It never invents or re-scores them.

Framework-aligned

Built on NIST CSF 2.0's six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. The same language your auditors already use.

Jira-ready backlog

Export the prioritized findings straight into a CSV your team can import into Jira. Findings don't die in a slide deck.

Why it matters

Boards and auditors expect evidence-based answers about security posture, not a spreadsheet someone updates once a year. Manual assessments are slow, inconsistent between reviewers, and hard to defend when someone asks "why is this the priority?" RiskLens turns a filled-out questionnaire into a scored, ranked, and explainable action plan in minutes, so the next conversation with leadership starts with an answer instead of a shrug.
